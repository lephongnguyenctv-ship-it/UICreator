# Diagnosis and Action Playbook

## Priority Rules

| Priority | Trigger | Interpretation | First action |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Core R2 drops by >= 2pp and Core count trends down | Core players are leaving. | Add reasons for regulars to return: events, goals, fresh content. |
| P1 | Core R2 < 50% | Existing-player retention is weak. | Repair return loop before scaling acquisition. |
| P1 | New R2 < 10% | First experience is not hooking players. | Fix tutorial, first session, and day-1 return motivation. |
| P1 | Overall R2 down >= 1.5pp, mostly rate-driven | Cohort quality declined. | Identify the weak cohort and fix its specific loop. |
| P2 | Core Survival and Core R2 both trend down | Core engagement depth is eroding. | Add content/challenges; review recent changes or nerfs. |
| P2 | Survival < 20 min | Sessions are shallow or end too quickly. | Improve pacing, mid-game goals, difficulty, and round length. |
| P2 | DAU down >= 10% over the analysis window | Material population decline. | Separate fewer arrivals from weaker retention. |
| P3 | Survival 20–39.9 min | Viable but below long-term target. | Improve depth toward 40+ minutes. |

## Metric Combination Reads

### High Survival Time + High R2

Strong engagement and retention. Scale exposure carefully, continue updates, and use the region/game as a benchmark.

### High Survival Time + Low R2

Players who do play are engaged, but too few return. Improve replay motivation, progression, missions, rewards, and the post-first-match journey.

### Low Survival Time + High R2

The loop brings players back but sessions are shallow. Investigate short rounds, early failure, weak pacing, limited depth, or an overly easy/hard difficulty curve.

### Low Match Count + High Survival Time

One match may be satisfying but replay is weak. Add replay incentives, post-match hooks, variety, and randomisation; assess whether matches are too tiring or long.

### Low Match Count + Low Survival Time + Low R2

The game is weak across depth, replay, and retention. Do not scale. Identify core gameplay/onboarding blockers and consider fast-fail or legacy status if iterations do not move the metrics.

## Action Quality

Every recommendation should identify:

1. the affected cohort or region;
2. the measurable weakness;
3. the likely product mechanism; and
4. the next metric expected to improve.
