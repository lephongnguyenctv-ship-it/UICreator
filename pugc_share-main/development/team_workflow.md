# PUGC VN — Team Workflow (Cadence)

> The PUGC VN team runs on a **1-week Agile sprint cycle**, with daily and
> weekly meetings. The **Thursday playtest** is a key component: it provides the
> feedback needed to refine gameplay and plan the next iteration.

## Recurring Meetings

| When | Meeting | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily, 9:30 AM** | Stand-Up | Status updates, today's targets, blocker identification. |
| **Monday, 14:00** | Weekly Update | Planning the week ahead & setting the target for the next milestone. |
| **Thursday, 14:00** | Play Test | Cross-team gameplay. Everyone plays & provides critical feedback. |
| **Last Friday, 14:00** | Monthly Brainstorming | Monthly strategic session to align on concepts for new maps. |

## Sprint Model

- **Cycle length:** 1 week (Agile sprint).
- **Feedback loop:** Thursday playtest → critical feedback → refine gameplay →
  plan the next iteration.
- **Planning:** Monday weekly update sets the milestone target; daily stand-up
  tracks progress and surfaces blockers.
