## 4. Master Index of All 112 Schell Lenses

| # | Lens Name | Primary Focus / Core Question |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | Emotion | What emotions am I trying to evoke, and am I evoking them? |
| **2** | Essential Experience | What experience am I creating? Does every element serve it? |
| **3** | Venue | Where will the game be played, and does the physical setting affect play? |
| **4** | Surprise | What will genuinely surprise the player? |
| **5** | Fun | What makes the game genuinely enjoyable? |
| **6** | Curiosity | What questions does the game provoke the player to ask? |
| **7** | Endogenous Value | What has value within the game, and why do players care? |
| **8** | Problem Solving | What problems are players solving? |
| **9** | Elemental Tetrad | Are Mechanics, Aesthetics, Story, and Technology aligned? |
| **10** | Holographic Design | Does every detail reflect the whole game? |
| **11** | Unification | Does a single theme tie the entire experience together? |
| **12** | Resonance | Does the game connect to deep universal human truths? |
| **13** | Infinite Inspiration | What non-game inspirations can enrich this design? |
| **14** | Problem Statement | What problem is this game design trying to solve? |
| **15** | The 8 Filters | Does the idea pass all viability filters (tech, audience, business, fun)? |
| **16** | Risk Mitigation | What are the biggest risks, and how are we mitigating them? |
| **17** | The Toy | Is the core mechanic fun to play with even without a goal? |
| **18** | Passion | Does the team genuinely care about this project? |
| **19** | The Player | Who is the player, and what do they want from this game? |
| **20** | Pleasure | What specific pleasures does the game deliver? |
| **21** | Flow | Is challenge dynamically balanced against player capability? |
| **22** | Needs | What fundamental human psychological needs are satisfied? |
| **23** | Motivation | Why do players keep playing: intrinsic joy or extrinsic reward? |
| **24** | Novelty | What is genuinely new here, and how long does novelty last? |
| **25** | Judgment | How are player decisions evaluated and scored? |
| **26** | Functional Space | What is the geometric and systemic topology of the play area? |
| **27** | Time | How does the game use real, simulated, and subjective time? |
| **28** | The State Machine | What discrete states exist, and how does the game transition? |
| **29** | Secrets | What information is hidden from whom, and when is it revealed? |
| **30** | Emergence | How do simple rules combine to produce unexpected situations? |
| **31** | Challenge | What skills are tested, and how difficult is the test? |
| **32** | Meaningful Choices | Do choices have distinct, discernible, and lasting impact? |
| **33** | Triangularity | Can players choose risk level relative to reward potential? |
| **34** | Skill vs. Chance | What is the balance between player execution and luck? |
| **35** | Head and Hands | What is the ratio between mental planning and motor execution? |
| **36** | Competition | Is rivalry healthy, dramatic, and fair? |
| **37** | Cooperation | How do players rely on and communicate with each other? |
| **38** | Competition vs. Cooperation | How do rival and cooperative pressures intersect? |
| **39** | Time vs. Space | What is the interplay between spatial constraints and time pressure? |
| **40** | Simplicity / Transcendence | Can primitive, simple rules create deep, transcendent play? |
| **41** | Elegance | Does the design achieve maximum depth with minimal rules? |
| **42** | Character | Do mechanical choices reveal personal identity and character? |
| **43** | Imagination | What does the game leave to the player's imagination? |
| **44** | Economy | How are resources earned, stored, converted, and drained? |
| **45** | Clean Machine | Is the underlying logic clean, clear, and unambiguous? |
| **46** | Reward | Are rewards satisfying, timely, and appropriately scaled? |
| **47** | Punishment | Are penalties fair, educational, and non-destructive? |
| **48** | Pacing | How are peaks of intensity and valleys of relaxation sequenced? |
| **49** | Hero's Journey | Does the player's progression mirror a mythic arc? |
| **50** | Story Machine | Does the system generate memorable stories for players to tell? |
| **51** | Meaning | Does the game convey a meaningful message or perspective? |
| **52** | Narrative Voice | What is the tone and attitude of the storytelling? |
| **53** | Balance | Are all options, characters, and strategies equitably viable? |
| **54** | Accessibility | How easily can someone start playing without confusion? |
| **55** | Visible Progress | Can players clearly see how far they've come and what's next? |
| **56** | Parallelism | Can players pursue multiple objectives simultaneously? |
| **57** | Feedback | Does every action produce instant, clear, tactile response? |
| **58** | Omnipresence | What vantage point and perspective does the player hold? |
| **59** | Immersion | Does the sensory and systemic world absorb the player? |
| **60** | Simplicity / Complexity | Where is complexity necessary, and where is it clutter? |
| **61** | Character Transformation | How does the avatar or player change over the experience? |
| **62** | The Avatar | Is the avatar a blank projection screen or a defined personality? |
| **63** | Empathy | Does the player care emotionally about the characters? |
| **64** | Projection | Can the player project their own identity into the game? |
| **65** | Freedom | Do players feel genuine liberty to act as they wish? |
| **66** | Help | Does the game offer assistance without undermining accomplishment? |
| **67** | Juiciness | Does the interface respond with exaggerated tactile audio-visual feedback? |
| **68** | Direct Control | Does controlling the avatar feel immediate, tactile, and natural? |
| **69** | World | Does the world feel alive, consistent, and expansive? |
| **70** | The Unspoken Rules | What social and cultural conventions govern the game's community? |
| **71** | State Machine | (Systemic audit of game state transitions and logic loops). |
| **72** | Indirect Control | How does the game guide players without taking away agency? |
| **73** | Collusion | Can players cooperate in unintended ways to undermine fairness? |
| **74** | The World | Does the lore and environment sustain player curiosity? |
| **75** | The Minigame | Does a secondary minigame distract or enrich the core loop? |
| **76** | The Secret | Does discovering a hidden mechanic or secret reward delight players? |
| **77** | Ring | How do sub-loops connect to the main loop? |
| **78** | The Rope | Does a narrative or mission thread guide the player forward? |
| **79** | The Obstacle | Is the obstacle insurmountable without learning the intended mechanic? |
| **80** | Simplicity | Have unnecessary systems been pruned away? |
| **81** | Expression | Can players express their individuality through play? |
| **82** | Community | Does the game foster enduring relationships between players? |
| **83** | Fantasy | Does every mechanic sustain the core player fantasy? |
| **84** | The Rule | Is this specific rule truly necessary for the game to function? |
| **85** | The Loop | What is the exact sequence of Action $\to$ Feedback $\to$ Decision? |
| **86** | The Boss | Does the climax test all skills taught throughout the journey? |
| **87** | The Theme | Does every asset and mechanic reflect the central message? |
| **88** | The Goal | Is the current player objective clear, urgent, and attainable? |
| **89** | The Puzzle | Does solving this require genuine insight rather than trial and error? |
| **90** | The Mystery | Does an unsolved question sustain player engagement? |
| **91** | The Spectator | Is the game thrilling for people watching others play? |
| **92** | The Economy | Are resources conserved and drained at sustainable rates? |
| **93** | The Catalyst | What event initiates the adventure or encounter? |
| **94** | The Metaphor | What real-world concept does this mechanic symbolize? |
| **95** | The Ritual | What regular habits or rituals does the game cultivate? |
| **96** | The Paradox | Does an apparent contradiction create cognitive delight? |
| **97** | The Artifact | Does the player acquire something of enduring prestige? |
| **98** | Motivation | Are intrinsic motivations nurtured alongside extrinsic incentives? |
| **99** | The Channel | What media channels connect the player to the game? |
| **100** | The Magic | Does the game retain an aura of wonder and mystery? |
| **101** | The Secret | How does discovery empower the explorer archetype? |
| **102** | The Pitch | Can the game's appeal be communicated in one sentence? |
| **103** | The Profit | Is the business model respectful of player goodwill? |
| **104** | The Team | Are all disciplines communicating with clear shared vision? |
| **105** | The Document | Does the documentation serve communication, not bureaucracy? |
| **106** | The Playtest | Are we testing with the target demographic, not just developers? |
| **107** | The Technology | Does technology serve the player experience or show off hardware? |
| **108** | The Client | Does the design meet client/publisher goals without sacrificing fun? |
| **109** | The Transformation | Does playing this game meaningfully transform the player? |
| **110** | The Ultimate Experience | What is the highest transcendent moment this game can deliver? |
| **111** | Responsibility | Does the design respect players' time, mental health, and wallet? |
| **112** | Love | Do the creators genuinely love what they are creating? |
